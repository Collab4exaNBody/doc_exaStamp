# **Reactive potentials**

Reactive (bond-order) potentials let chemical bonds break and form during the simulation: the strength of each bond depends on the local environment of the two atoms (coordination, bond angles, conjugation).

- [**REBO**](rebo.md): second-generation Reactive Empirical Bond Order potential for hydrocarbons (C, H).
- [**LCHBOP**](lchbop.md): Long-range Carbon-Hydrogen Bond Order Potential.
